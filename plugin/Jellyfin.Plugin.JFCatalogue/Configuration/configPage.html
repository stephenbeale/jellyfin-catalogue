JF Catalogue

An offline, searchable list of your library for your phone, so you can check what you already own while out shopping. Open the link below on your phone, add it to the home screen, and it keeps working with no signal.

Your catalogue link

Anyone with the link can see the catalogue (titles and details, not your media). Turn off to disable it.
Open it on your phone, then Share > Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or Install app (Android). Uses the address you're viewing this dashboard on: if your phone reaches the server by a different address, swap the start of the link for that.
